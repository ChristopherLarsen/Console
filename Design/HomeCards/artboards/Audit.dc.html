What the cards do today

Read from the shipped SwiftUI, not from the screenshot. Two of these are defects, not taste — they change what the user can read.

01
The session button sits on top of the priority
The ticket card is a ZStack(alignment: .topTrailing) whose second child — the terminal circle — lands exactly where row one puts Text(priority). Every ticket in the panel shows a truncated word: Lowe, Lu, Hi, Mediu. The field is rendered and then covered.

02
The colour is on the field nobody triages by
Priority gets the tint — red for Highest, orange for High — while status (Testing, In Progress, Backlog) is a neutral grey capsule. In a 16-row column the question is "what is moving and what is stuck", and that is status. Priority also carries almost no signal once twelve rows in a row read Low.

03
A box of boxes
Card fill and panel fill are the same colour — controlBackgroundColor — and the only thing separating them is a one-point separatorColor stroke. So sixteen hairline rectangles sit inside a hairline rectangle, and the panel reads as a grid rather than as a list. Contrast, not stroke, is how AppKit lists separate content from chrome.

04
The merge-request panels have two headers
HomeView turns the container header off for JIRA and Sessions only, so each MR panel renders the container's title and its own chrome header directly beneath it — the same words twice, and roughly thirty points of a panel that currently shows four of its rows.

05
Nothing is bigger than anything else
On macOS .footnote, .caption and .caption2 all land at ten points, so the ticket card's title, its status and its timestamp are the same size — hierarchy is carried by weight and grey alone. The title is the thing being scanned for; it should be the one element with size.

Also true, and cheaper to fix
Two time languages. JIRA cards print an absolute timestamp — Aug 21, 2026, 11:34 PM — while GitLab cards print "updated 4 hours ago". One dashboard, two ways of saying when.
A reserved line nobody uses. lineLimit(2, reservesSpace: true) plus minHeight 72 spends a second line on every one-line summary; four of sixteen tickets are visible.
A lonely eyebrow. When projectDisplayName is nil the MR eyebrow renders as "!5" alone on its own row — a whole line for two glyphs.
Author on every row of My MRs. The same component shows the author in both panels; in the authored list every row is the same person, so the field is pure noise there.
No hover state anywhere. Cards are buttons with .buttonStyle(.plain) and no hover, press or selection feedback — nothing tells you the row is clickable until you click it.
Two panels own headers, two do not. JIRA and Sessions build their own; the MR panels inherit one and build another. The dashboard has three header treatments.
