What changed, and where
Name becomes titleThe session name moves out of the trailing corner of row one and into the title slot at the card's fixed x-origin, at 13pt medium — the same position and size as a ticket summary or an MR title. HomeSessionsPanelView.swift:147–158
Subtitle rejoins the chipsFolder or summary and the artifact chips share one trailing row instead of two: folder in monospace on the left, chips right-aligned. The 56pt floor comes down to 44pt and a summary-less session is a two-line card. HomeSessionsPanelView.swift:160–172 · :193–221
Needs-you reads at a glanceStates that block the agent get a semibold state label and a one-point red inset on the card. HomeSessionsPresentation.needsYou already decides which those are — the card just has to show it. HomeSessionsPresentation.swift:35–44
Jump affordanceThe reserved trailing slot carries a chevron at 30% opacity — the same slot the ticket card gives its session button and the MR card gives its open glyph. Still one button; the glyph is decoration inside it, not a nested control, as the panel spec requires. CONSOLE_PANEL_2_SESSIONS.md:205
Chips stay chipsFilled capsules survive here and only here, because they mean a linked object — a ticket, an MR. That is why the ticket card's status capsule becomes plain tinted text: two different meanings should not share one shape.
Open questionSessions have no timestamp, so the age column at the right edge is empty on this card. Adding lastActivityAt to ConsoleSession would complete the column across all four panels — worth doing, but it is a model change and not part of a visual pass. SessionModels.swift:53
